About me

I'm a Senior Staff Machine Learning Engineer at Google X, where I lead the computer vision and multimodal AI work behind the Tapestry Grid Intelligence Platform. I received my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from Carnegie Mellon University and my B.S. in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Throughout my career I have worked across autonomous vehicles, robotics, geospatial mapping, agriculture technology, and energy infrastructure — including time at Waymo, Everyday Robots, Mineral.AI, and the Google Self-Driving Car Project — building machine learning systems that operate in complex real-world environments.

Research Interest

My mission is to build AI systems that help machines perceive, understand, and interact with the physical world. I have been approaching this from several angles: 1) computer vision and multimodal learning that combine visual, spatial, and sensor information at scale; 2) perception, localization, and sensor fusion for autonomous systems; and 3) decision-making and planning, including using large language models and vision-language models for robot reasoning. Most recently, I have been exploring foundation models and multimodal AI for real-world applications ranging from autonomous systems and robotics to crop intelligence and power-grid optimization.
