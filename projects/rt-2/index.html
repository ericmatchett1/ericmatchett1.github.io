RT-2: Vision-Language-Action Models

A single transformer that maps visual observations and natural-language instructions directly to robot actions — transferring web-scale knowledge into real-world control.

Everyday Robots · Google Research  ·  2023  ·  My role: contributor (vision-language-action modeling)

Vision-Language-ActionTransformersRobot ControlGeneralization

My contribution

I contributed to RT-2, focusing on vision-language-action modeling — connecting large pretrained vision-language models to real robot control so that web-scale knowledge could improve how a robot perceives and acts.

Vision-language-action

Unified perception, language, and action in a single end-to-end model.

Knowledge transfer

Brought web-scale semantic knowledge into robotic decision-making.

Action tokenization

Represented robot actions as tokens the transformer could generate.

Training & evaluation

Large-scale training and analysis of generalization to new objects and tasks.

Technical overview

  • Co-trains on web vision-language data and robot trajectories in one model.
  • Emits low-level robot actions as output tokens, like generating text.
  • Transfers semantic concepts from the web to unseen objects and instructions.
  • Removes the need for a separate perception → planning → control stack.

Results & impact

Emergent generalization

Handled novel objects and commands beyond the robot's training data.

Perceive and act in one model

A single network both understood the scene and produced actions.

Simpler pipeline

Folded multiple stages into one trainable system.
