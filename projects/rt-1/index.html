Project · Robot Learning

RT-1: Robotics Transformer for Real-World Control

A scalable transformer that learns generalizable manipulation skills from large, diverse real-robot demonstration data — mapping images and instructions to actions.

Everyday Robots · Google Research  ·  2022  ·  My role: contributor (model training & evaluation)

TransformersImitation LearningManipulationScalability

My contribution

I contributed to RT-1, focusing on training and evaluating a scalable robot-learning model — turning large, diverse demonstration datasets into a single policy that generalizes across many tasks.

Tokenized actions

Represented images, instructions, and actions for a transformer policy.

Learning at scale

Trained on large, diverse real-robot demonstrations.

Real-robot evaluation

Measured task success and generalization on physical robots.

Data pipelines

Built workflows to curate and scale demonstration data.

Technical overview

  • Maps camera images and a language instruction to discretized robot actions.
  • Uses a transformer backbone trained by imitation on broad demonstration data.
  • Scales with data and tasks, learning many skills in one policy.
  • Provided the control foundation later extended by RT-2.

Results & impact

Broad skill coverage

A single model performed a wide range of manipulation tasks.

Generalization

Transferred to new instructions and conditions.

Scalable recipe

Showed that more data and tasks improved performance.
