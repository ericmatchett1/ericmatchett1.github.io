My contribution

I lead the computer vision and multimodal AI work behind the Tapestry Grid Intelligence Platform — heading cross-functional teams that build the AI models and large-scale production systems supporting utility operations, from satellite imagery analysis to grid optimization.

Grid modeling

AI models that build a unified, high-fidelity view of grid assets and operations.

Power optimization

Models that predict grid behavior and help optimize power flow.

Asset intelligence

Computer vision on satellite/aerial imagery for asset detection and risk.

Foundation models & GNNs

Applied foundation models and graph neural networks across imagery and telemetry.

Large-scale infrastructure

Distributed training and data processing on cloud AI infrastructure.

Production ML

Turned research models into reliable systems supporting utility operations.

Technical overview

  • Fuses satellite/aerial imagery, sensor telemetry, and operational grid data.
  • Applies computer vision and foundation models to understand grid infrastructure.
  • Uses graph neural networks to reason over the structure of the network.
  • Backed by distributed training and large-scale cloud AI infrastructure.

Results & impact

Grid visibility

A unified view of assets and operations where data was previously fragmented.

Better decisions

More accurate asset analysis, network planning, and optimization.

Resilience at scale

Helps utilities manage renewables, rising demand, and aging infrastructure.
