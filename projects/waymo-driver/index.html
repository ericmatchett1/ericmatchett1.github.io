Waymo Driver: Perception & Sensor Fusion

Multimodal perception, behavior prediction, and decision-making that let an autonomous vehicle understand and safely navigate complex real-world environments.

Waymo  ·  2016–2019, 2023–2024  ·  My role: Tech Lead / Staff Software Engineer

PerceptionSensor FusionLiDAR · Radar · CameraLocalizationBehavior Prediction

My contribution

As Tech Lead and Staff Software Engineer, I led perception and sensor-fusion work for the Waymo Driver — building the models and systems that let the vehicle detect, track, predict, and respond to everything around it, and scaling them through distributed training and simulation.

Perception

360° understanding by fusing LiDAR, radar, and camera into one view.

Behavior prediction

Anticipated how other road users would move.

Localization & mapping

Centimeter-level positioning against HD maps where GPS falls short.

Decision-making

Systems for safe, natural driving choices in dense urban scenes.

Scale & simulation

Distributed training and AV simulation to accelerate and validate models.

Reliability

Improved detection reliability and cut false positives in busy driving.

Technical overview

  • No single sensor is enough — cameras struggle at night, LiDAR degrades in rain, radar is low-resolution.
  • A fusion layer combines LiDAR, radar, and camera into one reliable scene representation.
  • Prediction and decision-making models turn that understanding into safe actions.
  • Localization fuses GPS, IMU, and LiDAR against HD maps for precise positioning.

Results & impact

Reliable perception

Stronger object detection and tracking in challenging conditions.

Precise positioning

Centimeter-level localization in tunnels, urban canyons, and beyond.

Safe scaling

Supported safe expansion of ride-hailing across real-world environments.
