PaLM-E: An Embodied Multimodal Language Model

Injecting real-world images and sensor states directly into a large language model, so it can perceive the world and reason about it to produce plans for embodied tasks.

Everyday Robots · Google Research  ·  2023  ·  My role: contributor (multimodal grounding & reasoning)

View Official Project ↗ Read Paper ↗

My contribution

I contributed to PaLM-E, focusing on grounding a large language model in real-world perception — connecting visual and sensor inputs to language reasoning so a single model could both understand a scene and plan within it.

Multimodal fusion

Embedded images and states into the language model's token space.

Embodied reasoning

Reasoned over perception to produce grounded, executable plans.

Vision-language grounding

Linked language concepts to what the model actually saw.

Evaluation & transfer

Studied how skills transferred across robotics and vision-language tasks.

Technical overview

  • Encodes images and continuous sensor states as inputs to a language model.
  • Treats perception and language uniformly so one model handles many tasks.
  • Generates plans and answers grounded in the current physical context.
  • Benefits from scale and transfer across vision, language, and embodied tasks.

Results & impact

One model, many modalities

Unified vision, language, and embodied reasoning in a single system.

Grounded planning

Produced plans tied to real observations, not just plausible text.

Positive transfer

Training across domains improved capability rather than diluting it.
